# Product brief — current 0.8.0 experience baseline

## Goal and agreed scope

A private single-learner study workspace connects all configured lectures and available course
material to a source-first interface. Original Drive folders and stable source/learning identities
remain. The PC is the always-on execution/publication host; other devices use authorized portable
access and committed request/result exchange. All lectures remain in scope, not only pilot examples.
Unavailable, empty, unrecorded and unprocessed materials remain explicit rather than fabricated.

The learner opens a lecture, moves slide by slide, plays the relevant recording interval, follows
its transcript, inspects exact problems/readings/figures, asks for quick or deep help, explains their
understanding, receives correctable feedback, selects useful review and resumes later.

The latest user accepted the large-slide/context-panel direction, time-aware tutoring, minimal
relevant permitted data processing and no unapproved additional spending, and explicitly required
light and dark themes. This baseline includes Light / Dark / System and preserves original source
colors. See [UI decisions](ui-decisions.md), [site map](site-map.md), [all flows](ui-flows.md) and
[all stories](user-stories.md). Visual mockups are illustrative design references, not live evidence.

## Tutoring principle

Give a direct concise answer when requested. Mark that exchange not assessed instead of inferring
understanding or ignorance; preserve existing evidence. When the learner submits a real explanation
for assessment, capture it before reasoning, assess supported criteria under known help conditions,
record through the existing writer, and offer a short optional diagnostic. Deeper exploration is a
learner choice. No fabricated learner replies, compulsory quizzes, new attempts from regrading, or
permanent misconception labels based on one ambiguous response.

## Existing authority and runtime limits

Preserve original materials, `learning/skills.yaml`, family/rubric records, BE 505's existing question
index, immutable attempts/corrections and current tracking/shared owners. Concept trees, gap summaries
and due lists are projections. Complete new turn/message sidecars and scheduling/resume events are
operational extensions with explicit ownership. Do not introduce another canonical memory database.

Native OpenAI/ChatGPT remains the preference with exact product/surface honestly identified. Current
reports establish candidate Codex interfaces, not an independent verified desktop-thread roundtrip.
UI state must expose that blocker. No app-managed billed inference or alternate provider is silently
substituted. Later Claude compatibility is allowed by the logical interface, not certified here.

## Definition of success

Less time finding source context and a useful complete study loop without lost responses, false
source links, misleading learning records or uncontrolled cost. Accepted matching/assessment quality
and practical latency need real measured evidence. More generated cards, messages or passing starter
tests do not demonstrate learning gains. Current prototype/specification checks do not test the app.

The first private release is not a public SaaS, social network, grade editor, scheduler of personal
Calendar/Tasks, or rewritten academic archive. Existing planning and coursework restrictions retain
their owners. Product design does not grant permission to install services, expose ports, transmit
restricted material, change provider settings or publish canonical records.
